Blokir Ads is a premium, open-source Android application that blocks digital advertisements in other apps (games, utilities, etc.) using a local DNS VPN mechanism — safely and privately. It operates entirely on your device without requiring root access.

<b>Key Features:</b>
<ul>
  <li><b>Per-App Targeting:</b> Choose exactly which apps you want to block ads in. The VPN tunnel only intercepts traffic from your selected apps.</li>
  <li><b>Local Processing (Privacy First):</b> All DNS interception happens locally. Your data is never sent to a third-party VPN server.</li>
  <li><b>Multi-Layer Blocklist:</b> Ships with a built-in static blocklist covering 30+ major ad networks, plus an auto-updated dynamic list of 100,000+ domains (Hagezi Multi Pro, Steven Black, AdGuard Mobile, Regional Asia).</li>
  <li><b>Smart Whitelist:</b> Protects reward/points systems, CDN video streams, Firebase, and payment gateways from being accidentally blocked.</li>
  <li><b>Live DNS Terminal:</b> Watch blocked connections in real time through an in-app terminal UI.</li>
  <li><b>Tap-to-Block Custom Domains:</b> If an ad slips through, tap the suspicious domain in the terminal to permanently block it.</li>
</ul>

<b>Disclaimer:</b> Because this app uses DNS-based blocking, it cannot block First-Party Ads (such as YouTube pre-roll ads or Instagram sponsored posts) since those ads are served from the same domain as the actual content.
